The Program

9 Months That Change Everything

At Skillence Nextgen Institute, our program is carefully designed to equip you with real-world, high-income digital and professional skills. The session runs for 9 months, consisting of:

  • 7 months of intensive hands-on practical training and real-life project experience
  • 2 months focused on advanced practicals, personal branding, and marketing strategies to help you monetize your skills and begin earning confidently
Commitment Fee

One Fee. Every Scholarship Category.

Students awarded the Full Scholarship have their tuition completely covered. However, all admitted students — including beneficiaries of the Full Scholarship, 80% Scholarship, and 50% Scholarship categories — are required to pay a Commitment Fee.

₦15,000
Required from all admitted students across every scholarship category, as a sign of commitment and readiness to grow.

This fee helps support:

  • Learning materials and resources
  • Training infrastructure and student support systems
  • Administrative and onboarding processes

Most importantly, it serves as a sign of commitment and seriousness — because every scholarship slot has been sponsored by individuals and partners who believe in empowering ambitious young people like you.

Outstanding Balance

For 80% & 50% Scholarship Students

Students admitted under the 80% Scholarship and 50% Scholarship categories are also expected to complete payment of their outstanding tuition balance on or before the deadline below, to secure their admission and scholarship benefits.
